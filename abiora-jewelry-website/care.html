KEEP IT BEAUTIFUL

Jewelry Care Guide

A few simple habits can help your favorite pieces stay beautiful for longer.

01

Keep it dry

Remove jewelry before showering, swimming or exercising. Let it dry completely before storing.

02

Avoid chemicals

Perfume, lotions, hairspray and harsh cleaning products can affect jewelry finishes.

03

Store properly

Keep pieces in a dry pouch or jewelry box, ideally separated to prevent scratches and tangles.

04

Clean gently

Use a soft, dry microfiber cloth. Avoid abrasive cleaners and rough brushing.
